Tapdown Tycoon

Clients: Toronto Argonauts & Coinbase

Overview:

Tapdown Tycoon is a cookie-clicker x management simulation inspired game where players build and manager their own stadium! Players earn coins through tapping, upgrading buildings and staff to expand their empire!

Coinbase was looking for a game concept that aligns with their brand while raising cryptocurrency awareness amongst audience interested in exploring it. We proposed a clicker x tycoon game that combined coin-earning mechanics with resource management to showcase themes of growth, investment and expansion.

After the success of the first run in 2025, Coinbase returned for another year, looking for new ways to re-engage new and returning players. We expanded the gameplay to include a new mini-game, in-game market and challenge based tasks.

I was an artist and designer in this project. I developed visual concepts, game assets, UI and marketing materials.

Role: Artist & Designer, Visual Production, Marketing Materials

Tools: Figma, After Effects

Team: Danny Tran, Ruqian Da, Nic Bradford, Daniel Houseley, Eric Dickinson
