Ultra Striker

Clients: Toronto FC & LG

Overview:

Ultra Striker was a promotional paper-toss-style arcade game in featuring multiple difficulty modes. Following the success of its first run, LG brought the game back for a second iteration with new game features.

LG came to the team with the goal of increasing their brand awareness for three of their home appliances. Each appliance was designed with attention to real-world accuracy, with consideration given to which items would realistically belong to each appliance. Many featured tossable items maintained consistency with the TFC branding.

Level difficulty was tailored to each appliance's design in order to create unique gameplay experiences that complimented both brands.

My role as a designer for this project was lead artist. I developed visual concepts, game assets, UI and marketing materials.

Role: Lead Artist & Designer - Concept, Visual Production, Marketing Materials

Tools: After Effects, Figma

Team: Fahad Edwards, Nic Bradford, Eric Dickinson
