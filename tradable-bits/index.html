MLSE ARCADE x Tradable Bits

Clients: MLSE ARCADE x Tradable Bits

Overview:

MLSE Arcade collaborated with Tradable Bits to create a collection of customizable games developed for brand partners that offered adaptable gameplay experiences that could be reskinned to reflect each partner's visual identity.

My contributions spanned across multiple projects, designing to have partners easily collaborate and incorporate their own branding and campaign elements through reskinning.

Role: Artist & Designer - Concept, Visual & Asset Production

Tools: Figma, Illustrator, Clip Studio Paint

Team: Derrick Matthews, Danny Tran, Daniel Houseley, Nic Bradford,
Chidozie Ikpe, Becky Wu
