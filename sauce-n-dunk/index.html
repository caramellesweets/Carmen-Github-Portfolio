Sauce N' Dunk

Clients: Toronto Raptors & Hellmann's

Overview:

Sauce N' Dunk is a sidescrolling platformer game where the Raptor is dunking on specially designed Hellmann's Mayos that each have their own unique superpowers that block the Raptor's shot!

Hellmann's was looking for opportunities to promote their upcoming flavours to increase brand awareness. We personified each flavour into a unique character. Each mayo's superpower is designed around the intensity and distinct taste profile of each mayo flavour along with their different environmental biomes.

My role as a designer for this project was lead artist. I developed visual concepts, game assets, UI and marketing materials.

Role: Lead Artist & Designer - Concept, Visual Production, Marketing Materials

Tools: Aesprite, After Effects, Figma

Team: Fahad Edwards, Ruqian Da, Nic Bradford, Daniel Houseley, Eric Dickinson
