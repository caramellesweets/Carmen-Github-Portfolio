Crowd Connect

Clients: Toronto Raptors & Bell

Overview:

Crowd Connect is a real-time, In-Arena showdown game where the audience in the arena are divided into two opposing teams for a face off in a tug-of-war style battle. Using their mobile devices, players tap as fast as possible to ensure their team's raptor pulls the wire to their phones to stay connected!

Bell was looking for a way to create a high-energy, interactive experience to showcase its brand during the short home-game intermissions. It was a 2-3 minute experience that encouraged fans to connect with everyone else in the arena through their mobile devices.

I was an artist and designer in this project. I developed visual concepts, game assets, UI and marketing materials.

Role: Artist & Designer, Visual Production, Marketing Materials

Tools: Figma, After Effects, Photoshop

Team: Danny Tran, Ruqian Da, Nic Bradford, Eric Dickinson
