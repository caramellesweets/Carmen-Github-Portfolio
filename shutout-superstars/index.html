Shutout Superstars

Clients: Toronto Maple Leafs & PointsBet

Overview:

Shutout Superstars was a fast paced hockey goalie game where players move their goalie left and right to block incoming pucks. Players must react quickly to incoming shots while avoiding red-herring items such as snowballs to achieve the highest score.

This game was initially inspired by an existing game developed for another client, but required a different vibe and feel to make it more unique to the new client. The visual direction underwent several rounds of design exploration before reaching its final look with the sleek vector art style to match PointsBet's aesthetic.

Role: Artist & Designer - Concept, Visual & Asset Production, Contest-Page

Tools: Figma, Illustrator, Clip Studio Paint

Team: Derrick Matthews, Ruqian Da, Chidozie Ikpe, Nic Bradford,
Daniel Houseley, Eric Dickinson
