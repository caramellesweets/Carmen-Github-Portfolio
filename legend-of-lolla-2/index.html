Legend of Lolla 2

Clients: MLSE ARCADE x Lollapalooza

Overview:

Legend of Lolla 2 was a retro-inspired dance battle game where players battle opponents using dance moves as their attacks.

Inspired by classic Pokémon-style battle RPGs, the game featured unique characters and environments influenced by Chicago, the host city of that year's Lollapalooza festival.

I collaborated with fellow artists and developers on the team as a character artist, animator, environment/concept designer, contributing to the development of a multitude of characters, game environments and animations.

Role: Artist - Concept, Character, Environment, Animation

Tools: Aesprite, Figma, Procreate

Team: Nic Bradford, Daniel Houseley, Becky Wu

Year: 2023
